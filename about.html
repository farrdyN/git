I am a student. Beneath the soft glow of a streetlamp, a stray cat quietly watched the world go by. The night air was cool and still, filled only with the faint rustle of leaves and the distant hum of a passing car. Somewhere nearby, laughter echoed from a balcony, reminding the cat that life continued in a thousand quiet ways. It blinked slowly, stretched its legs, and padded off into the shadows—unseen, unnoticed, yet very much a part of the night’s gentle rhythm.
